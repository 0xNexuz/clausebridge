# ClauseBridge proposal

The original proposal this build follows, kept verbatim so every feature can be checked against it.

_ClauseBridge App Proposal - by 0xNexuz_

Contract and obligation management built with Univer

I propose building ClauseBridge, an application that helps small
businesses and project teams turn contracts into organized obligations,
deadlines and payment trackers.

## The problem

Agreements are frequently stored as separate PDFs or Word files.
Important delivery dates, payment conditions and responsibilities are
then copied into spreadsheets or forgotten entirely. This makes it
difficult to see what has been promised, what is due and what has been
completed.

## The product

Users would upload contracts and review AI-suggested obligations in a
shared workspace. ClauseBridge would let users:

- Extract suggested deadlines, deliverables, parties and payment terms for human confirmation.

- Track each approved obligation with an owner, status and supporting evidence.

- View upcoming deadlines, outstanding payments and unresolved items.

- Prepare progress summaries and management reports linked to the contract text.

Users would confirm extracted terms before they become tracked
obligations. The app would support contract administration, not replace
legal review or offer legal advice.

## Who would use it

Initial users would be small agencies, procurement teams, consultants,
startups and organizations handling multiple supplier or service
agreements.

## How Univer fits

Univer Docs would provide the editable contract review workspace with
clear references to source clauses. Univer's relational tables, where
available under the chosen SDK plan, would organize obligations and
statuses. Univer Sheets would handle payment schedules and totals, and
Univer Slides or Docs could present progress reports. ClauseBridge would
supply the extraction pipeline, human review, reminders and audit
history around these tools.

## Proposed collaboration

For a collaboration, I would deliver a functional application, the
GitHub repository and setup guide, an end-to-end demonstration, and
public build updates on X. I would also welcome Univer's advice on
document features and commercial licensing.
