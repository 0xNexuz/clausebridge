SUPPLY FRAMEWORK AGREEMENT

This Supply Framework Agreement is dated 1 October 2026 (the "Commencement Date") and is made between Northgate Logistics Ltd of Ikeja, Lagos (the "Buyer") and Clearview Office Supplies Ltd of Yaba, Lagos (the "Supplier").

1. Term
1.1 This Agreement runs for twelve (12) months from the Commencement Date.
1.2 The Buyer shall tell the Supplier in writing whether it wishes to renew no later than thirty (30) days before the end of the Term.

2. Orders and Delivery
2.1 The Supplier shall deliver the first quarterly stock order to the Buyer's Ikeja warehouse by 15 October 2026.
2.2 The Supplier shall deliver the second quarterly stock order by 15 January 2027.
2.3 For any additional order, the Supplier shall deliver within ten (10) business days of receiving the Buyer's purchase order.

3. Prices and Payment
3.1 Each quarterly stock order is priced at NGN 1,250,000 including delivery.
3.2 The Buyer shall pay NGN 1,250,000 for the first quarterly order by 14 November 2026.
3.3 The Buyer shall pay NGN 1,250,000 for the second quarterly order by 14 February 2027.
3.4 The Supplier may review its prices once a year by giving the Buyer at least sixty (60) days' written notice.

4. Quality
4.1 The Buyer shall inspect each delivery and report any shortage or damage to the Supplier within two (2) business days of delivery.
4.2 The Supplier shall replace damaged or defective items within five (5) business days of being notified.

5. Compliance
5.1 The Supplier shall provide the Buyer with a valid tax clearance certificate by 31 December 2026.
5.2 The Supplier shall keep accurate delivery records for every order and share them with the Buyer on request.

6. Termination
6.1 Either party may end this Agreement by giving sixty (60) days' written notice.

Signed for Northgate Logistics Ltd: Tunde Bakare, Procurement Manager
Signed for Clearview Office Supplies Ltd: Ngozi Eze, Sales Director
