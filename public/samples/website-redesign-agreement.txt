WEBSITE REDESIGN SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Website Redesign Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 3 November 2026 (the "Effective Date") between Harbor & Pine Foods Ltd, a company registered in Lagos, Nigeria (the "Client"), and Brightline Studio Ltd, a design agency registered in Accra, Ghana (the "Agency").

1. Services
1.1 The Agency will redesign and rebuild the Client's public website as described in Schedule A (the "Services").
1.2 The Agency shall name a project lead and notify the Client of that person within five (5) business days of the Effective Date.

2. Deliverables and Timeline
2.1 The Agency shall deliver a discovery report summarising user research and site analytics by 20 November 2026.
2.2 The Client shall provide all final page copy, product photographs and brand assets no later than 27 November 2026.
2.3 The Agency shall deliver high-fidelity design mockups for the home, menu, locations and contact pages by 11 December 2026.
2.4 The Client shall approve the design mockups or request changes within ten (10) business days of receiving them.
2.5 The Agency shall launch the completed website on the Client's hosting by 29 January 2027.

3. Fees and Payment
3.1 The total fee for the Services is USD 15,000.
3.2 The Client shall pay a deposit of USD 4,500 within seven (7) days of the Effective Date.
3.3 The Client shall pay USD 6,000 on 18 December 2026, following delivery of the design mockups.
3.4 The Client shall pay the final instalment of USD 4,500 by 12 February 2027.
3.5 Invoices are payable by bank transfer. Late payments carry interest of 1.5% per month.

4. Support and Hosting
4.1 For three (3) months after launch the Agency shall fix reported defects within two (2) business days of each report.
4.2 The Client shall renew its domain name and hosting plan before they expire on 31 March 2027.

5. Reporting
5.1 The Agency shall send the Client a written status report on the first business day of each month.

6. Insurance
6.1 The Agency shall maintain professional indemnity insurance of at least USD 250,000 for the duration of this Agreement.

7. Termination
7.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving thirty (30) days' written notice to the other party.

8. Governing Law
8.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.

Schedule A - Scope of Services
The new website will include a home page, a menu page with online ordering links, a locations page for the Client's four restaurants and a contact form. The Agency will build the site on the Client's existing hosting and hand over editing access and a short training session at launch.
